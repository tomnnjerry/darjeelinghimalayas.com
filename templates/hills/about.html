About us

We would rather show you the bill

Darjeeling Himalayas plans trips in Darjeeling, Kalimpong, Sikkim, the Singalila Ridge and the Dooars for people who want to see the hills properly without paying luxury prices.

Most hill packages are sold as a single number. We think you should see what is inside it: the jeep seat, the homestay, the permit, the guide. So every trip on this site carries its fare broken into lines, and every quote we send does the same.

We also think a place is better when you know its story. Who built the monastery, why the railway loops, what the name of the village means in Lepcha or Nepali. Each place page starts there, then gets practical.
